The guard validates the existing **NVS board identity**. It does not physically identify the PCB or verify its wiring. Confirm the PCB revision printed on the board and its existing reported identity before following [installation](installation.md). A missing, unreadable or unsupported identity stops normal startup before hardware initialization; the firmware does not infer a supported identity from a similar device name.

Inherited source definitions do not extend support. This Gamma release does not contain a QAxe/OctAxe firmware image or independent per-chip mining. Its opt-in tuner adjusts the whole miner within the reported hardware/user constraints; a successful build or another board's bench does not establish physical qualification.

The earlier Gamma 601 a1, Beta 2, Beta 3 and [Beta 4 observations](validation-beta4.md) apply to those earlier image pairs. [Beta9](validation-beta9.md), [Beta7](validation-beta7.md) and [Beta6](validation-beta6.md) retain their own separate evidence. Those observations and the later Beta10 checks do not qualify Beta11. Target compilation, host regression tests and OLED rendering are separate evidence. The owner confirmed normal physical screens and fans after final Beta10 installation. Candidate art/motion remain simulated fixture evidence; controlled cold power-cycles, changed operating points, sustained soak and complete fault recovery remain outside the current short bench. The test MUX advertises fresh status to both units; this Gamma receiver handles the legacy acknowledgment, not the QAxe's richer coin/block metadata.

The [previous Gamma Beta10 pair](https://github.com/WillItMod/5tratumFW/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.10) remains available. Clear the Ethernet setting and save, power off and disconnect the module before returning to both Beta10 files over Wi-Fi.

Use a matched application and WWW pair from one build. Preserve the original operating point through compatible OTA updates; the firmware retains existing protection behavior, including changes that can follow overheating. Ordinary operating writes retain the Beta9-compatible journal when authentication matches the original raw-key baseline; changing the new authentication values away from that baseline uses a format Beta9 cannot read. See [upstream migration](upstream-migration-20261028.md), [settings preservation](settings-preservation.md) and [recovery](recovery.md) before attempting rollback or recovery from an identity or storage fault.
